PCO Stage Plot

A bookmark that builds and maintains Stage Plot AM and Stage Plot PM on the Bentonville Stage Plot. Create places everyone the first time, Update keeps the plots in step with the schedule without moving anyone you've placed, and Reorient evens out the spacing when you want it.

← All production tools

Install (about a minute)

Works in Chrome on a computer.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar

    Press Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac or Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows.

  2. Drag this button onto the bar

    Stage PlotClick and hold the button, drag it up to your bookmarks bar, and let go.

    If the button won't drag into your bar, use one of the other ways to add it at the bottom of this page.

  3. Open a plan and click it

    Go to the plan you want in Planning Center Services, then click Stage Plot in your bookmarks bar. From any other Services page it uses the next upcoming plan. You need to be signed in to Planning Center.

How To Use It

Three buttons

Click the bookmark on a plan and a small panel opens in the corner with Create, Update and Reorient. AM covers the 8:45 and 10:30 people, PM the 4:30 people.

Create

Builds both plots from scratch with everyone placed roughly where they stand. Use it once per plan. If the plots already exist it asks first, because starting over wipes your hand adjustments.

Update

Adds newly scheduled people and removes anyone who declined or was taken off. Nobody already on the plot moves. A replacement takes the exact spot of the person they replaced, so a new bass player lands where the bass was.

Reorient

Evens out the spacing of the front line (singers, with any instrument they play) and the bottom row, keeping everyone in their current left-to-right order. The back line stays put.

Who goes where

Drums center back, bass to its left, other instruments around the back. Singers form the front line: guitar singers left, keys singers right, vocalists in the middle. Leaders bottom left, Production Coordinator bottom center, WC Tech bottom right.

Fine-tune

Click a plot's name in the panel to open it in the stage editor and drag anyone into place. Your adjustments survive every Update. Reload the plan page to see new plots in its Files list.

Print Packet

The plots are named exactly what the Print Packet looks for, so they show up on the back of the Audio, GFX, Lighting and Livestream reports.
